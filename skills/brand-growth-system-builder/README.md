## What it is

How a business looks and how it is found: the tokens every asset is built from, the mark
and its rights, the printed and digital collateral, the local and directory visibility,
and the social and email channels that carry it.

## When to use it

- "we need a logo and brand guidelines"
- "our cards and letterhead look different at every printer"
- "nobody can find us locally"
- "which social accounts should we even be on"
- "can we use this image we found"
- "we need a deck for the investor meeting"

## How it starts

Works out which of the three jobs the request is - **make it consistent**, **make it
findable**, or **make it credible** - then hands off to the one or two modules that do it.
It never builds an asset itself.

> **Q:** Is this about how the business looks, or about how it gets found?

- **Identity** - What is the business called, and what does one line of it do? / Is there a
  mark today, and who made it? / Any name or trading-name change coming?
- **Assets** - Where is the brand used - sign, vehicle, invoice, app, uniform? / Which
  formats are needed, web or print? / Does the editable file still exist?
- **Visibility** - Should customers find you by place, by service, or both? / Which
  directories or citations already exist? / Is a Business Profile claimed?
- **Channels** - Which accounts are live, and what is each one for? / Who approves a post
  before it goes out?
- **Constraints** - Any brand assets already licensed, and do the rights sit with the
  business? / Anything that must not be published?

## The order

```
Tokens decided
  -> Mark drawn, rights recorded
    -> Collateral built from the mark
      -> Site register written
        -> Local profile and citations aligned
          -> Channels carrying the same identity
            -> Deck, email and social reusing the same assets
```

Tokens come first because everything downstream inherits them. Site and citations come
before channels, because a channel that sends people somewhere unaligned undoes the work.

## Rules every module holds to

- A token is recorded once. If two assets disagree, the token is wrong, not the assets.
- Clear space and minimum size are usage rules, not preferences.
- Every asset carries its source, rights owner and licence. "We found it online" is not a
  source.
- Name, address and phone must be identical across the profile, the site and every
  citation. A mismatch costs more than a missing listing earns.
- A trademark search happens before the mark is drawn, not after it is used.
- Free does not mean unrestricted. Commercial use and attribution are separate answers.
- A deck slide carries the sensitivity marking and the data source, not just the number.

## Owner and cadence

- Owner: whoever owns the brand, with a named approver for trademark use.
- Cadence: the router runs per request; each module reviews its schema when the brand,
  the channels or the licence terms change, not on a fixed date.

## Troubleshooting

- The route is wrong - answer the consistency/findable/credible question literally; the
  order above is the route.
- Two modules look equally right - pick the one upstream. Everything else inherits it.
- Notion columns show as Text - expected, apply the mapping table in the module skill.
- A field count differs between SKILL.md and the page - the SKILL.md Field Reference is
  canonical; the page is generated from it.
