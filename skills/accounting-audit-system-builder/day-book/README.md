## What it is

The daily cash, bank and digital receipt-and-payment record, reviewed and reconciled at day end

## When to use it

- day book
- daily cash and bank book
- cash book and bank book
- daily receipts and payments register
- end of day balance check

## How it starts

Opens with one question, then asks only what is still missing - one per message.

> **Q:** What does your cash and bank record look like at the end of a day today?

- **Books** - Cash, bank and digital all separate? / Which software or format? / Daily or
  weekly entries?
- **Entries** - Who writes them? / From vouchers or from the bank feed? / Same day or next?
- **Balances** - Opening and closing balance carried daily? / Verified against statement?
  / Who signs off?
- **Controls** - Duplicate check done? / Missing entries spotted how? / Digital wallets counted?
- **Outcome** - What do you need? / A daily book, a reconciliation record or both?

**Recommended approach:** One day book with a row per receipt and payment, a book section for cash, bank or digital, a running balance per book, and a day-end row that totals the day across all three books and verifies the balances and checks for duplicates before the day is closed.

**Workflow:** Receipt or payment recorded from voucher → Booked to cash, bank or digital → Running balance updated → Duplicate and missing entries checked → Day-end balance verified and reviewed

Artifacts are built only after the user asks for them.

## SQL setup

```sql
CREATE TABLE day_book (
  entry_number VARCHAR(255),
  entry_date DATE NOT NULL,
  book_section VARCHAR(100) NOT NULL,
  transaction_reference VARCHAR(255),
  voucher_number VARCHAR(255),
  party VARCHAR(255),
  narration VARCHAR(255),
  mode VARCHAR(100) NOT NULL,
  cash_in NUMERIC(14,2) NOT NULL,
  cash_out NUMERIC(14,2) NOT NULL,

  bank_in NUMERIC(14,2) NOT NULL,
```

## Owner and cadence

- Owner: whoever writes the book daily, with a separate reviewer signing off at day end.
- Cadence: entries on the day of the movement; balances verified and reviewed at day end, bank agreed to the statement at least monthly.
- Data: keep the example row until the first real record replaces it.

## Relations

Link fields: `Source Documents`

## Troubleshooting

- Columns show as Text after import - expected, apply the mapping table.
- Relation column stays empty - the target database must exist first.
- Dates sort as text - convert the column to Date after import.
- Someone asks for Debit and Credit columns - the presentation follows the software's day-book format; record it in `Debit/Credit Presentation` instead of hardcoding a rule.
- The day's movements are spread over three books - a `Day Summary` row totals cash, bank and digital together, with `Mode` = `Mixed`.
- Running balance will not tie - check for a duplicate transaction reference or a payment booked in the wrong book section.
- Day is still open - `Balance Verified` = `Pending` is the correct value, not `Yes`.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the difference between day book and a spreadsheet?

A spreadsheet is a container. This is a defined set of 25 fields with a named owner - whoever writes the book daily, plus a separate reviewer signing off at day end - and a review rhythm of one close per day rather than one per week. The same 25 fields are emitted as CSV, SQL, JSON Schema and a Notion mapping, so the four cannot disagree.

### What fields does a day book system usually include?

At minimum Entry Number, Entry Date, Book Section, Transaction Reference and Mode. A complete version also covers Cash In, Cash Out, Cash Balance, Bank In, Bank Out, Bank Balance, Digital In, Digital Out, Duplicate Check and Balance Verified. This template ships all 25, with a `Day Summary` row totalling the three books at day end.

### Do I need day book if my team is small?

Yes - a starter-stage system, and most small teams need it early. The deciding question is not headcount, it is whether anyone can answer "what was the bank balance at close yesterday, and was any payment entered twice" without checking with a person first.

### What does this system not do?

It ships an empty template, and it does not:
- post entries, move money or connect to a bank feed.
- settle the debit/credit presentation. That follows whatever your own software's day-book format produces, which is what `Debit/Credit Presentation` records.
- prove the entries were correct. It records what was entered; the proof is the linked source document.
- run the bank statement reconciliation. It records that the reconciliation was done, not what it found.

### How does it fit with other systems?

It links rather than duplicates. `receipt-accounting` holds the detail behind the receipt lines, `payment-accounting` the detail behind the payment lines, `petty-cash-management` the float the cash book section is counting, and `source-document-filing` the target behind `Source Documents`. Each keeps its own table and joins on an ID, so any one of them can be replaced without breaking the rest.
