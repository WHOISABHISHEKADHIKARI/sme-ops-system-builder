## What it is

Money received, allocated to the right account, and reconciled to cash or bank

## When to use it

- receipt register
- receipt voucher book
- money received log
- collections tracker
- bank receipt reconciliation sheet

## How it starts

Opens with one question, then asks only what is still missing - one per message.

> **Q:** How do you record money received today?

- **Receipts** - How many in a month? / Cash, bank, cheque or digital? / Any part-payments?
- **Allocation** - Allocated to invoices? / Advances or loans mixed in? / Who allocates it?
- **Evidence** - Receipt voucher or bank advice? / Issued to the customer? / Filed where?
- **Reconciliation** - Bank reconciled how often? / Cash counted? / Differences handled how?
- **Outcome** - What do you need? / A receipts register, a reconciliation view or both?

**Recommended approach:** One receipt record carrying the mode, the gross received, the receipt type, the invoice it clears and the unapplied balance, and post it to income only when the type says it is a sale.

**Workflow:** Money received → Mode and source recorded → Typed as collection, advance, loan or capital → Allocated to an invoice or held unapplied → Reconciled to bank or cash

Artifacts are built only after the user asks for them.

## Owner and cadence

- Owner: whoever records receipts day to day.
- Cadence: every receipt on the day it is received; reconciliation status reviewed with the bank or cash count.
- Data: keep the example row until the first real record replaces it.

## Relations

Link fields: `Sales Invoice Allocated`, `Source Document`

## Troubleshooting

- Columns show as Text after import - expected, apply the mapping table.
- Relation column stays empty - the target database must exist first.
- Dates sort as text - convert the column to Date after import.
- Row will not balance - check that gross received equals amount allocated plus unapplied amount.
- `TDS Collected on Receipt` is not extra money - it is the part of the allocation that is remitted, so the balance check above still holds.
- Bank account filled in full - record it masked, for example `HDFC Current ****0042`.
- A receipt looks like income but is not - set `Receipt Type` and `Treats as Sale Income` before the entry.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the difference between receipt accounting and a spreadsheet?

A spreadsheet is a container. This is a defined set of 23 fields with a named owner - whoever records receipts day to day - and a stated review rhythm against the bank or the cash count. the same 23 fields are emitted as CSV, SQL, JSON Schema and a Notion mapping, so the four cannot disagree.

### What fields does a receipt accounting system usually include?

At minimum Receipt Number, Receipt Date, Received From, Receipt Mode and Gross Amount Received. A complete version also covers Receipt Type, Treats as Sale Income, Amount Allocated, Unapplied Amount, TDS Collected on Receipt and Reconciliation Status. This template ships all 23, with gross received equalling amount allocated plus unapplied amount on every row.

### Do I need receipt accounting if my team is small?

Yes - a starter-stage system, and most small teams need it early. The deciding question is not headcount, it is whether anyone can answer "how much came in this month, and how much of it is still unapplied" without checking with a person first.

### What does this system not do?

It ships an empty template, and it does not:
- post entries, raise invoices or compute tax.
- decide the accounting treatment. The ledger, the tax position and the period all matter, and a qualified accountant confirms them.
- chase an unapplied balance. It records it and carries it forward until somebody identifies it.
- connect to a bank feed or move money.

### How does it fit with other systems?

It links rather than duplicates. `sales-accounting` holds the invoice behind `Sales Invoice Allocated`, `payment-accounting` is the other half of the cash and bank movement, `party-ledger-reconciliation` proves the debtor balance once the money is allocated, and `day-book` is the daily book these receipts land in. Each keeps its own table and joins on an ID, so any one of them can be replaced without breaking the rest.
